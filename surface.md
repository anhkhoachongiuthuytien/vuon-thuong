# Garden

Mode: Experience.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An open pastel sky with soft clouds. Small italic dedication upper left; centered “Từ một bông hoa nhỏ…” above one layered pink rose on a long green stem. The rose is a real keyboard/touch button with a meaningful label.

Signature: The rose's touch causes a timed growth wave across a garden of pink, peach, mint and blue layered flowers. Tall flowers at the back, larger blooms toward the bottom; the original central rose stays identifiable. At the climax a cream letter opens.

States: ready, growing, garden, letter. Repeat garden must cancel prior timers and reuse assets. Letter opens and closes without resetting garden; replay restarts whole choreography. Keyboard escape closes letter, Tab contained by native dialog, focus restores to visible controls.

QUALITY BAR: reference-like soft petal rims and shadowed folds, organic stems and layered depth. No flat spiral symbol, dashboard framing, screenshot background, generic confetti, abrupt population jump or giant controls.
